# Field data / Datos de campo

`muestreos_numerados.csv`: 365 records from 43 samples of benthic macroinvertebrates, as written in the original
spreadsheets (spelling mistakes included, since they are part of what the paper tests).

- **Argentine Puna** (Puna argentina), sites 1–12, about 4000 m a.s.l.; three campaigns (autumn, spring, summer).
- **Western Chaco** (Chaco occidental), sites 13–15; three seasons.
- Sampling: D-frame net, 120 µm mesh; 1 m² of bottom disturbed for 1 minute; two replicates per site, pooled.
- Localities are withheld; sites are numbered. Abundance = individuals in the pooled sample.

Columns: `region`, `estacion` (season), `sitio`, `taxon_como_en_la_planilla`, `abundancia`.
